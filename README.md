# Evidence: PR #275 Article resolution P2 fixes, paired live-model measurement

Evidence for [HUM-CDCH/FREE#275](https://github.com/HUM-CDCH/FREE/pull/275), the follow-up to #267 that fixes the
two P2s left open at #267's merge: a REPEAT beside an equal item labelled OTHER (P2-1), and long lists left
unresolved for their budget (P2-2). This branch holds evidence only; it is not meant to be merged.

## State

- **Measurement verdict: not accepted under the sealed protocol.** C1 (the PR #267 criteria A1-A4 on the 12
  annotated papers) fails in both conditions, on A1: the Baltic cod paper lost its gold row. C2, S1, S2 and S3 hold
  in both conditions. See [`measurement/results.md`](measurement/results.md).
- **The C1 failure is not caused by #275.** The accepted resolver (on `dev` at `811bb9f2`) fails identically on the
  same readings. In both conditions the reasoning model labelled every item of the Baltic cod list OTHER, including
  the paper's own backbone collagen, and resolution emptied the list.
- **P2-2 works where it applies.** On 4 generated long-list documents, the accepted resolver refused 5 list parts for
  budget and kept all 46 cited copies. The fix resolved every list in parts that fit, removed all 46 cited copies and
  deleted none of 265 own objects, in both conditions.
- **P2-1 was not exercised.** No counted run used the REPEAT label (672 OWN, 111 OTHER, 0 REPEAT).
- PR #275 stays draft. Per the protocol, no fix-and-rerun cycle follows without the maintainer.

## Open points

1. **Whole-list OTHER on the Baltic cod paper, in the default-on resolver on `dev`.** Both conditions removed the
   paper's entire own data. A1 counts it as one row. The model's labels on this paper have changed in every execution
   (N kept 5, 4, 4, 0 of 12; Q kept 1, 1, 3, 0 of 3). It needs a maintainer decision before `dev` is promoted to
   `main`.
2. **Accepting #275 is the maintainer's call.** By the sealed rule it is not accepted. The failing criterion fails
   identically without the change, and the change's own criteria (C2, S1-S3) hold.
3. **P2-1 has no live evidence** beyond the uncounted pilot 3. It rests on the unit tests.
4. **Deferred review follow-ups** (they change `resolution.py`): P3-2, the halving search's CPU cost; P3-3, the other
   parts of a list that cannot be settled are still sent (needs its own check); P3-4, a comment on `if fitting:`.
5. **Not run at `358fdde0`:** the Postgres, live-model test, service and system tiers, and checks on the merged
   result with current `dev`.
6. **Limits:** fourth execution on the same 12 papers, and generated synthetic inputs (`p2s-a` was seen in pilots).
   A run on papers outside the corpus is still recommended.

## Contents

- `measurement/protocol.md`: the protocol, sealed before any counted model call.
- `measurement/seal.json`: SHA-256 of every input and script, and both resolver blobs. Paths in it are relative to
  the measurement folder as it was run: `../pr267-resolution-comparison/score.py` is `comparison/score.py` on
  [`evidence/pr267-resolution-comparison`](https://github.com/HUM-CDCH/FREE/tree/evidence/pr267-resolution-comparison)
  (SHA-256 `c22c74c3…`), and `synthetic/documents/*` are regenerated by the generator (below).
- `measurement/harness/`: `run_paired.py` (one reading per run, both resolvers on the same assembled input),
  `resolution_b.py` (the accepted resolver's exact bytes, blob `5a284de0`), the two schemas, and `parse_pdf.py`.
- `measurement/launch/`: the run and queue scripts used on the GPU host.
- `measurement/score_p2s.py`: the scorer. It runs PR #267's `score.py` unchanged for the corpus.
- `measurement/synthetic/generate_p2s.py` and `spec.json`: the generator and its spec.
  `python generate_p2s.py spec.json OUT_DIR`, with the synthetic PDF benchmark at `1513c446` importable, reproduces
  all 4 `truth.json` and `source.pdf` files byte for byte (checked against `seal.json`).
- `measurement/results.md`: the verdict, every measure, and the adjudication of every dropped item.
- `measurement/withheld.sha256`: SHA-256 of what is not published.

## Withheld

The repository is public, so the run captures, `report.json` and the corpus gold are not published: the captures
hold long excerpts of the source papers, and the gold is a held-out set. Their hashes are in
`measurement/withheld.sha256`; the files are kept with the maintainer.
