# Labeling notes: conrad-2011-bbc-graves-de

- Shape: pp1-2 are a literature catalogue of old Bell Beaker finds (numbered sites 1-24, no Bef. numbers); p2-3 the rules of the new-finds catalogue ('7.3. Katalog Neufunde', Beigaben vs Funde definition, measures of features in m, finds in cm, weights in g); pp3-16 per-site records 'Grab/Befund/Pfostengrube N (Bef. X) - Abb. Y' for Kölsa, Grebehna, Löbnitz-Bennewitz, Markranstädt, Zwenkau, Großstorkwitz (four codes), Wehlitz. Sampled entries all sit on pp9-16; p8 and p12 are figure-label residue.
- All 19 sampled records exist in the document; none were merged or invented. Record 0 'Grab 3 (Bef. 22)' is Löbnitz-Bennewitz (p9), not Kölsa 'Grab 8 (Bef. 22)' (p6): Bef. numbers repeat across sites, so figure_reference in context was needed to disambiguate.
- The extractor's title 'Archäologische Grabungsberichte: ...' is invented; the document carries no title, only the heading 'Gräber' and section '7.3. Katalog Neufunde'.
- OCR damage: the Markranstädt Grab 1 Beigaben continuation and Pfostengrube 1 (p10) are lower-cased and split into two anchors ('Unverz. gefäß', 'rotlehmfragmente'); Wehlitz 'Beigaben: 1) V erz. Glockenbecher' (p16) is split mid-word. Long item catalogues are single anchors (Zwenkau Grab 1 Beigaben is one 14 kB anchor), so grave_goods anchors are coarse.
- Systematic extractor problem: grave_goods was filled from Funde (finds from the fill) and from non-grave features (post holes, a Befund without burial), although p3 defines Beigaben and Funde as distinct categories. Four grave_goods strings are composed labels (counts, material glosses, motif summaries) rather than the catalogue wording: labelled (c).
- excavation_year 2007/2006 for the ZAX-19/20 records is the date of a Feldbegehung (surface find), accepted as the year of recovery.
- DOUBT: [4] records[1].cremated_bone_weight_g 416.3: p10 gives 'Gew. 295' for the Leichenbrand and 'Gew. 416,3' for a second lot '~2/3 mit Feinkies durchsetzt'; 416.3 is stated as a Leichenbrand weight but is mostly gravel. Labelled (b).
- DOUBT: [9] records[2].grave_goods 'Rotlehmfragmente' (Pfostengrube 1): stated under 'Funde:' of a post hole, not a Beigabe. Labelled (b).
- DOUBT: [26] records[7].grave_goods '2 verz. Glockenbecher' (Befund 1, Bef. 95-186/3): 'zwei verz. Glockenbecher (1, 2)' is stated but under 'Funde:' of a feature without burial. Labelled (b).
- DOUBT: [28] records[7].grave_goods 'Silexklinge' (Befund 1): 'Funde: ... 6) Silexklinge'. Labelled (b).
- DOUBT: [42] records[12].grave_goods 'Silexabschlag' (Grab 1, Bef. 128): 'Funde: ... 5) Silexabschlag', a find from the fill. Labelled (b).
- If grave_goods is meant to cover all listed objects regardless of Beigaben/Funde, flip entries 9, 26, 28, 42 to supported with the anchors quoted in their notes (entries 27 stays (c) as a paraphrase).
