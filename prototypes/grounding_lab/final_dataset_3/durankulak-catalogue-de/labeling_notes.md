# Labeling notes — durankulak-catalogue-de (60-entry sample)

- Document: "Katalog der prähistorischen Gräber von Durankulak" (Todorova et al.), 58 pages. Page 1 is the symbol legend (7 = Säugling, # = Kind Gr. I, ( = gestört, g = Kenotaph, F = Totenmahlkomplex). Pages 2-58 are one paragraph per grave: `GRAB <n> <symbols> . 1 -<Planquadrat>; h-<depth>. <stone construction>. <body position> Typ ..; Orient. ..; <sex>, <age class> <age range> Jahre. -Inv.:` followed by inventory lines with `( Tabl. x,y )` references and a trailing culture/phase attribution. All sample records sit on pages 50, 53-55, 57-58 (anchors 3464-3968).
- Culture attribution is TRAILING: the `Hamangia-Kultur, Stufe III.` / `Varna-Kultur, Stufen II-III.` paragraphs (rendered as `##` headings in document.md) belong to the grave whose inventory precedes them, not the one that follows (decisive case: the heading before GRAB 1077 "Nicht näher datierbar"). Culture/phase gold anchors for 1081, 1114, 1117A, 1194, 1194 A, 1202 are such trailing paragraphs.
- Conventions applied as "typed form", not paraphrase: Mann/Frau -> Male/Female; Mat./Ad./Sen./Juv./Säugling/Kind -> Mature/Adult/Senior/Juvenile/Infant/Child (legend on p1); `h-1,10` -> 1.1; age_min/age_max taken from a stated range `50-55 Jahre` (field name demands the endpoint); a single stated age `40 Jahre` gives min = max = 40. If the caller wants range endpoints as (c), entries 2, 19, 26, 58 flip.
- table_reference values are `; `-joined lists of the `Tabl.` refs in the inventory lines; when the refs come from several inventory lines all those anchors are listed as gold (entries 11: 2 anchors, 32: 4 anchors). The extractor's ordering matches the document's.
- OCR damage: `V arna-Kultur` (space inside the word) for graves 1124, 1138, 1140, 1143A, 1190, 1121; treated as stating Varna-Kultur. Some inventories are split across 3 anchors around a `Tabl.` reference (1130, 1143A, 1194), and the anchor for grave 1150 is followed by a stray `, Am Kopf goldener Ohrring k2710` line that belongs to no visible grave.
- Extractor record problems: lettered graves lost their letter. Record 51 (context: h 1.3, Infant, Tabl. 185,4) is GRAB 1117A but carries grave_number 1117, colliding with record 50 (the real GRAB 1117). Record 108 (h 1.2, Inf. I 0 Jahre, Tabl. 196,6) is GRAB 1194 A but carries 1194, colliding with record 107 (the real GRAB 1194). Their other fields (culture etc.) were labeled against the lettered grave, which does exist in the document.
- No invented or merged graves found in the sample; every context matched exactly one catalogue paragraph.
- DOUBT: entry 23 (records[51].grave_number = 1117): 1117 could be read as the numeric part of the label 1117A (schema has a separate grave_label), but the document numbers this record 1117A; labeled (b).
- DOUBT: entry 54 (records[108].grave_number = 1194): same situation with GRAB 1194 A; labeled (b).
