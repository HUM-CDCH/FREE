# Labeling notes: dobes-1998-kugelamphoren-de

- Document: M. Dobeš, "Gräber der Kugelamphorenkultur in Nordwestböhmen", Saarbrücker Studien und Materialien zur Altertumskunde 6/7 (1997/98), Bonn 1998. Pages used: p1-2 (title, byline), p2-20 (Fundliste 2.1-2.6: ~45 grave/site entries with Fundstelle, Fundumstände, Beigaben, Aufbewahrung, Lit.), p21-27 (figure captions), p28-43 (analysis, chronology; comparison sites in Germany named in prose), p43-48 (bibliography).
- The 60-entry sample is almost entirely `records[i].site` / `records[i].district`; no grave_goods measurements were sampled. Records with index > ~21 are not catalogue entries: the extractor harvested every place name from the analysis chapters and the bibliography, including regions (Niedersachsen, Altmark, Uckermark, Nordwestböhmen, Ostböhmen, Schlesien, Jütische Halbinsel, Polska) and museums/districts (Litoměřice, Teplice (Museum)) as `site` -> all labeled (b).
- Policy applied: a site value is supported when the document's own text (heading, Abb. 1 legend, Table 1 row, caption, or prose) names the place as a find location, even if it is only a comparison site from the literature (Baalberg, Menz, Dahme...). Values whose only occurrence is inside a cited title in the bibliography are labeled (b) (a cited work's statement, not the document's), with DOUBT where the cited title gives exactly the claimed district. Punctuation/hyphenation-only differences (WeißandtGölzau, Schmiede-berg, Kutná Hora, 'Dänemark') are accepted; letter-level differences (Cítolib/Cítoliby, Zličov/Zlíchov, Großohringen/Großobringen) are not.
- Title and author are wrong: the title is a composed string from section headings 7-8 (a); the author string embeds a derivation note instead of the byline 'Miroslav DOBEŠ (Most)' p2 (c).
- Invented/merged records: Weschnitz, Bez. Kaaden (= Běšice, Kr. Chomutov, via Preidel 1937a title); Podersam (Berg Rubin) (= Dolanky, Berg 'Rubin'); Neratovice (Chemie-Kombinat Spolany) and district 'u Mělníku' (from Kabát/Zápotocký 1962 title; catalogue says Neratovice, Kr. Mělník); Zámků u Bohnic (= Praha-Bohnice); Praha 5-Zličov (= Praha-Zlíchov); Horka u Kučlína, Hrádek u Čáslavi, Vraný 'na Slánsku', Toušeň 'okr. Praha-východ' all copied from Czech bibliography titles.
- OCR: heading numbers are spaced oddly ('2 .1.2 Blšany'); háčeks are often lost (Blsany, Brandys, Zernoseky, ZIichov, Zlfchov, Verv ary); line-break hyphens dropped or turned into soft hyphens (WeißandtGölzau, Schmiede- berg, bie- ten); the Beran/Beier 1994 title is split so 'Ldkr.' and 'Stendal' sit in different anchors. Table 1 (p34) cells survive as table_cell anchors but the numeric columns are mostly empty.
- DOUBT [1] author: byline anchor states Dobeš; rejected only because the typed value adds a derivation note.
- DOUBT [23] Ldkr. Stendal: cited title gives this district for Dahlen, but bibliography-only and split across two anchors; document prose says 'Dahlen bei Stendal'.
- DOUBT [34] Kr. Wernigerode: cited title (Stolle 1986) gives exactly this district for Benzingerode; bibliography-only.
- DOUBT [37] okr. Teplice: cited title (Petrlík/Zápotocký 1992) gives this district for Horka u Kučlína; bibliography-only.
- DOUBT [40] Cítolib: same village as catalogue '2.3.1 Cítoliby, Kr. Louny'; the emitted form is the citation's.
- DOUBT [46] okr. Praha-východ: document heading has 'Toušeň, Kr. Praha-východ' - same district, German abbreviation.
- DOUBT [51] Kr. Weimar: cited title (Walter 1991) gives this district for Großohringen; document prose says 'Großobringen in Thüringen'.
